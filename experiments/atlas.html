ATLAS is a local analytical workspace, not a brokerage, custodial feed, automated financial adviser, or trading platform. CSV positions are a point-in-time snapshot. Cost basis and today's gain/loss are not historical performance. Historical risk depends on complete synchronized market data; expected returns depend on editable assumptions.
