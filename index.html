How to read these signals

SMA20/50/200 use 20/50/200 observed closes. RSI14 uses Wilder smoothing, seeded with the first 14 changes; above 70 indicates strong/extended momentum and below 30 weak/oversold momentum, neither guarantees reversal. MACD is EMA12 minus EMA26, with an EMA9 signal seeded from its first 9 valid values. Relative volume compares the latest volume with the previous 20 observed sessions. Indicators use all loaded history before the chart window is selected. Prices are unadjusted and splits/dividends may distort signals. Missing history is never filled.
