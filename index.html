How to read these signals

SMA20/50/200 use 20/50/200 observed closes. RSI14 uses Wilder smoothing, seeded with the first 14 changes; above 70 indicates strong/extended momentum and below 30 weak/oversold momentum, neither guarantees reversal. MACD is EMA12 minus EMA26, with an EMA9 signal seeded from its first 9 valid values. Relative volume compares the latest volume with the previous 20 observed sessions. Indicators use all loaded history before the chart window is selected. Prices are unadjusted and splits/dividends may distort signals. Bollinger Bands use SMA20 ±2 population standard deviations of the last 20 closes; touching a band does not establish a reversal. Drawdown uses the selected window’s running highest close. The prior 20-session closing range excludes the latest close and is context, not a guaranteed support/resistance level. RSI and MACD are calculated before filtering the display window. Missing history is never filled. Indicator education ↗

PUBLISHERS / RELEASE CALENDARS

Follow the release, not the refresh.

Economic readings arrive weekly, monthly or quarterly. A fresh snapshot can correctly show an older reference period. The updater checks available publications hourly during weekday release hours; “Check published snapshot” reloads that file. It does not request a new agency release.

BLS releases ↗BEA releases ↗Census indicators ↗DOL weekly claims ↗Federal Reserve releases ↗FRED release calendar ↗

Latest available data can be revised and is not a point-in-time archive. Monthly dates identify the month; quarterly dates identify the quarter’s start. Inflation is calculated from published seasonally adjusted indices; GDP growth is annualized quarter over quarter. Daily policy rates are effective market rates, not the FOMC target range. Missing releases remain missing. No consensus estimates, surprise scores or inferred release dates.
