MARKET DESK / MEASUREMENTS BEFORE STORIES

What the data supports.

Deterministic statistical and economic context. Observations are separate from interpretations; correlated indicators do not count as independent confirmation.

Interpretation limits

These are dated daily closes and latest available macro releases. There is no live order flow, causal model, consensus surprise, calibrated forecast or AI-generated explanation. Covered-stock breadth is a small, selected universe; it is not NYSE breadth. Economic releases are revised and their reference dates are not their release timestamps, so this page is not a point-in-time macro backtest.

FUNDAMENTAL LAB / SEC FILINGS

Company accounts, made inspectable.

Consolidated reported financials, formulas and editable valuation scenarios. Missing tags stay missing; funds are separated from operating companies.

FCFF scenario DCF

Five-year constant-growth scenario, then a perpetual-growth terminal value. Enter normalized unlevered free cash flow; OCF − capex shown above is not automatically treated as FCFF. All assumptions below are yours, not forecasts.

Verify debt, non-operating assets, dilution, share classes and valuation date. This simple enterprise bridge omits minority interests and other claims. Generic FCFF is disabled for financial firms and REITs.

Dividend discount scenario

Available only when a positive annual per-share dividend is reported. You must judge its sustainability; a reported dividend alone does not establish model suitability.

Scope & source

SEC standard US-GAAP facts only, from matching filing accession and exact reporting period. Custom segment disclosures, footnotes, off-balance-sheet commitments, accounting-policy judgments and qualitative competitive analysis need the source filing. Annual/quarterly periods are separate; missing Q4 is not manufactured. Ratios are a starting screen, not a complete company audit. Financial firms need sector-specific analysis.
